## Reading the market

**The same title can mean different career stages.** Palantir recruits graduating students into forward-deployed software engineering. OpenAI uses a similar title for a role requiring seven years of experience. PagerDuty’s version is a staff role for engineers who can let an agent act safely during a production incident. Search for both *forward deployed* and *customer engineering*, then check the experience line and travel expectations.

**An AI job can have a familiar title.** GPU cluster operations, clinical evaluations, and accounting agents appear here under operations engineer, software engineer, and new-grad software engineer. Useful search terms from the postings include *fleet reliability*, *Kubernetes control plane*, *agent orchestration*, *detection and response*, *AI monitoring*, and *health evaluations*.

**How to interpret growth.** BLS estimated that computer and mathematical occupations accounted for 3.5% of employment in the New York–Newark–Jersey City metro in May 2024. That local measure describes the size of a broad workforce. The national projections on the [growth evidence page](./outlook) measure expected change over time. These 19 postings show which duties and qualifications employers associate with emerging specialties. Estimating local growth for an individual title would require repeated, deduplicated observations over time. [BLS metropolitan employment report](https://www.bls.gov/regions/northeast/news-release/2025/occupationalemploymentandwages_newyork_20250429.htm)

**Posting status.** Employer recruitment pages listed these jobs when reviewed. Follow the source to check availability before applying. Career-stage headings use stated eligibility, years, or responsibility where an employer gives no formal level. Infrastructure and adoption roles are identified by the part of an AI system or rollout they support.
