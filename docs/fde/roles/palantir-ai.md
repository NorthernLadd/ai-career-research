## Work and responsibilities

Help customers decide where generative AI can improve their operations, then build and deploy those workflows. Small teams own delivery and use lessons from customer projects to improve Palantir’s Artificial Intelligence Platform.

- Translate a customer problem into a feasible AI workflow.
- Build LLM applications, data pipelines, and analytics for production use.
- Work directly with users and revise the system as objectives change.
- Feed recurring requirements and implementation lessons into the platform team.

## Skills and eligibility

- Past experience building LLM solutions and understanding the generative AI landscape.
- Machine-learning foundations in evaluation, training, and breaking problems into solvable parts.
- Strong engineering background and programming proficiency, with Python, Java, C++, or TypeScript/JavaScript as examples.
- Interest and ability to travel to customers.

## Preferences and supporting experience
