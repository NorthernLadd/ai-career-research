## Work and responsibilities

Join a customer delivery team for a year, building software and AI applications around operational problems. The FDE track combines customer discovery, solution architecture, and implementation. Students receive ownership of consequential projects and are assessed on delivery and impact.

- Understand the customer’s data, decisions, and operational constraints.
- Build and deliver applications using Palantir platforms and AI capabilities.
- Own implementation from initial problem definition through customer use.
- Collaborate within a small engineering team and explain technical choices.

## Skills and eligibility

- Pursuing or holding an engineering degree, with relevant technical disciplines preferred.
- Programming proficiency in a language such as Python, Java, C++, or TypeScript/JavaScript.
- Hands-on AI work through coursework, projects, internships, or extracurricular activities.

## Preferences and supporting experience

- Familiarity with data structures, storage, cloud infrastructure, and frontend tools.
- Security clearance or eligibility is a plus for the commercial track. The posting states a clearance requirement for the separate government track.

## Fit and source

Freshmen through seniors are explicitly welcomed. The one-year duration is a substantial academic scheduling commitment.
