## Work and responsibilities

Work in a small engineering team embedded with commercial customers. Start by understanding an operational problem, then develop the data integration, application, or language-model workflow needed to address it. The intern takes a project from customer conversations through deployment and works with both everyday users and executive stakeholders.

- Make architecture and design decisions with other engineers.
- Prepare large datasets and connect them to custom applications and LLM workflows.
- Iterate on a production solution as users reveal constraints or change priorities.
- Manage customer relationships and explain how the delivered software affects their operations.

## Skills and eligibility

- Engineering degree, with technical fields such as CS, electrical/computer engineering, mathematics, physics, or data science preferred.
- Proficiency in at least one language, with Python, Java, C++, and TypeScript/JavaScript offered as examples.
- Graduation in December 2027 or spring 2028.

## Preferences and supporting experience

- Comfort with data structures, storage, cloud infrastructure, or frontend frameworks.
- Independent problem solving when requirements are incomplete.
- Clear collaboration with technical and nontechnical users.
