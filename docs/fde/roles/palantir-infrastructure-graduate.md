## Work and responsibilities

Operate customer deployments of Palantir services and improve their reliability. Work with product and deployment engineers on systems design, production incidents, migrations, and upgrades. The role uses automation, including AI tools, to reduce manual operational work.

- Maintain monitoring, alerts, configuration, and software upgrades.
- Deploy products and migrate installations to newer infrastructure.
- Diagnose incidents and improve reliability and scalability.
- Automate repetitive tasks and runbooks, including work in Foundry and Apollo.

## Skills and eligibility

- A strong engineering background and demonstrated programming proficiency.

- Active clearance or eligibility and willingness to obtain it.

## Preferences and supporting experience

- Automation and scripting, including Bash where appropriate.
