# Sources and review method

Reviewed November 12, 2026. Profiles use employer careers pages and their linked applicant systems. Searches supplied leads, then Chrome was used to read duties, qualifications, location, and application availability.

## Interpreting status

**Application link or form present** means the employer page offered an application route when inspected. Hiring progress, headcount, and eventual acceptance of applications require employer confirmation.

**Uncertain** means the employer page contains conflicting evidence. Citi requisition 261002851 displays a November 11 posting date and an October 23 anticipated closing date.

Each employer link in a profile is the source for that profile. The shared skills guide is a synthesis. Portfolio exercises are editorial suggestions. Tool examples retain their status as examples or preferences.

## Coverage and limits

The sample includes four internships, two explicitly graduate roles, and six experienced roles across seven employers. New York dominates the sample. Citi supplies a Jersey City example with uncertain availability. The sample supports comparison of roles and requirements. Estimating growth or the size of the local FDE labor market requires observations over time.

Pay is recorded using the employer’s units. Monthly and hourly internship rates require hours and duration to estimate total earnings. Google’s displayed pay range has an unclear period. Equity, bonuses, and benefits vary by offer.

## Excluded and ambiguous leads

- Hatz AI’s indexed NYC FDE listing returned “Job not found” on the employer’s application host. It was excluded from the profiles. [Checked listing](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/hatz-ai/c6f8bdbf-1630-4b03-903a-781165e1185e)
- Palantir’s Warp Speed internship appeared in its board under a full-time work-type label. The dedicated internships included here had consistent internship labels. [Employer board](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir?location=New+York%2C+NY)
- Teleskope’s live page specified three years of experience and a $140,000–$180,000 base range. An older search snippet showed two years and $130,000–$170,000. The profile follows the live employer page. [Teleskope source](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/teleskope/278bce22-653b-41f1-aaeb-2c2f7c7f4092)

## Updating a profile

Revisit the exact employer URL. Check the title, location, work type, eligibility, working arrangements, qualifications, and deadline. Record the new review date and changes in the repository history. If the page disappears, retain the useful research with an explicit historical status and remove it from any current-opening summary.
